PRE-BLOOM

  1. Put 1 cup water into a Large Bowl
  2. Add 1 tablespoon Dry-Active Instant Yeast
  3. Add 1 tablespoon White Sugar
  4. Let Dr-Active Instant Yeast bloom for 10 minutes or until bubbles rise

MIXING

  1. Pour 6 cups All-purpose flour into a bowl
  2. Gently begin your previous mixture and then 2 additional cups of water
  3. As you mix in water mix in 1 additional tablespoon of sugar, 1 tablespoon of salt, 1 tablespoon of olive oil
  4. Once it forms a less-than sticky ball that can hold its shape, cover it in a bowl leave for as long as desired to rise. If over one or multiple nights, refridgerate.

BUILDING

  1. Begin letting your oven preaheat. Around 500 degrees F can be ideal, but lower temperatures can suffice if needed
  2. Spread out your dough as far as possible or to the limits of what you are putting it on in the oven.
  3. Shake out an amount of garlic, olive oil, basil, oregano, and red pepper to taste
  4. Recommendation to add a pinch of salt and two pinches of sugar to the can before pouring out have the can onto your pizza and spreading to the edges. Store the rest of the sauce refridgerated in a seperate container than the can.
  5. Break down the cheese as small as possible with whatever tools you have available. Spread cheese over pizza
  6. Put pizza into oven for ten minutes and stand by watching. Add or subtract time based on preference.
  7. Don't be afraid to ask for help when needed.

If you have extra dough or lower appetite, the dough can easily be made into Breadsticks!
